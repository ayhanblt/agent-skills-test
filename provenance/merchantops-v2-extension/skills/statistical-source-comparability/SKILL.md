---
name: statistical-source-comparability
description: Verify whether public statistics support comparisons across places, periods, or sources. Use when preparing evidence-based comparisons or interpreting apparent changes.
---

1. Translate the request into the population, geography, time window, measure, unit, and operation required. Locate the official data and its reference metadata.
2. Record the data release, table identifier, filters, and source URLs. Extract observations with their status flags. Preserve missing values and distinguish estimates from observed values.
3. Check whether definitions, coverage, methodology, and units match across the proposed comparisons. Look for series breaks, reporting changes, denominator changes, and changes in spatial coverage. Consult producer notes when the general metadata does not resolve a flagged observation.
4. Compute changes from comparable observations with explicit units. Report blocked or qualified comparisons with the exact reason and source. Tie the headline claim to the evidence and keep descriptive differences separate from causal explanations.
