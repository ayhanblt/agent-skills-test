Export createTransfer(storage,{size,sha256},afterCommit=()=>{}). Same transfer contract as T5. Additionally storage.read() gives durable bytes; commit(buffer) atomically writes and may throw BEFORE commit. afterCommit() runs after every NEW successful commit, before ACK (write return), and may throw a simulated crash AFTER disk commit. Recreate instance after crash; identical retransmission must ACK durable offset without another commit or afterCommit call. Do not store offset only in memory, truncate bytes, duplicate data or mask commit/crash errors. Multiple instances may interleave synchronous calls against the same adapter; read current committed state for each operation. finish verifies bytes from storage now, not a cached offset/hash. No new dependencies, external stores or changes to storage adapter.
