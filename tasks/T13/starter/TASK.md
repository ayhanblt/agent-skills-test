A local event registration app has a one-seat competition between two people. Deliver a standalone Playwright test.mjs, executed as node test.mjs with BASE_URL and PILOT_CHROME from the environment. Both isolated browser contexts should see the available seat before either books it. When both try to book, exactly one person should get a confirmed reservation and the other should get sold-out. A reliable test should wait for visible terminal states, close its browser and exit non-zero when this behavior fails. The evaluator runs the test against correct, delayed-correct and double-booking implementations. It records whether the two contexts and both POSTs actually happened. A local CLI and Playwright are supplied. Do not alter the app. No external browsing.
